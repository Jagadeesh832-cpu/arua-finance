
🚀 Introducing AURA FINANCE — AI-Powered Personal Finance Platform

Excited to share AURA FINANCE, an AI-powered personal finance and taxation platform built during our hackathon journey at Dr. K.V. Subba Reddy Institute of Technology (KVSRT).

Our goal was to make personal finance simpler, smarter, and easier to understand, especially for young adults and first-time investors.

✨ Key Features

🤖 AI Money Coach — Personalized financial guidance based on income, expenses, goals, and risk preferences.

📊 Financial Health Score — A 0–100 view of overall financial health.

💰 Smart Budgeting — Budget creation and spending-limit tracking.

🧾 Expense Tracker & Insights — Track and analyze spending patterns.

🎯 Financial Goal Planner — Set and monitor financial goals.

📈 Investment Simulator — Explore SIP, FD, PPF, and investment scenarios.

🛡️ Emergency Fund Calculator — Estimate required emergency savings.

🧮 Smart Tax Planner — Compare tax regimes and explore tax-saving opportunities.

📑 Monthly AI Financial Report — Personalized financial summaries and recommendations.

🔔 Smart Spending Alerts — Alerts when spending approaches or exceeds budget limits.

🧠 AI & Technology

We integrated Google Gemini for AI-powered financial assistance, recommendations, and reports, with a focus on providing simple and practical explanations.

We also used Google Antigravity during development to accelerate implementation, debugging, and development workflows.

🛠️ Tech Stack

React • Vite • JavaScript • Tailwind CSS • Node.js • Express.js • MongoDB • JWT • bcrypt • Google Gemini • Recharts

💡 What We Learned

Building AURA FINANCE within a limited hackathon timeframe gave us hands-on experience in full-stack development, AI integration, APIs, databases, authentication, data visualization, deployment, debugging, and teamwork.

The experience taught us that building a good product isn’t just about adding features — solving the right problem and making the core experience reliable matters just as much.
